Why I check everything

My mother kept a little notebook by the stove. Only the things that really worked went inside.

A stain that came out. A sauce with no lumps. The right way to keep parsley. If a trick failed twice, she crossed it out with one firm line. I still remember the sound of the pencil.

Today the tricks arrive on a phone, faster than any of us can test them. Some are wonderful. Many are only good for the camera. So I do what she did: I try it, I find the reason, and only then I write it down.

That notebook is what I share with you here.

Bisous, Odette
